Yuanyuan Gao | 高源远

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Prof. Dan Xu. I received my bachelor's and master's degrees from the School of Automation at Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU), where I was a member of BRAIN Lab, advised by Prof. Dingwen Zhang and Prof. Junwei Han. I have also had the pleasure of working with Zhihang Zhong and Xiao Sun.

My research aims to build scalable, cost-effective 3D/4D data pipelines for creating large-scale, realistic simulation environments. By bridging the real-to-sim gap, I seek to advance embodied intelligence and spatial understanding in vision-language models (VLMs).
